Guided Assistance 1.0.0

- A native document knowledge assistant for iPhone and iPad, installable through AltStore Classic.
- Import your own searchable PDFs, text, and Markdown. Browse passages and open original PDFs.
- Create request guides with checklists, steps, suggested wording, and references.
- Add new facts and procedure updates with effective dates, review status, and revision history.
- Optional on-device AI chat with source citations and an explicit approximately 3 GB model download. No cloud AI fallback.
- Optional device authentication and encrypted exports for manual backup or transfer.

The app starts empty; your documents are not part of the repository or app download. Local AI performance still depends on your physical device's memory and must be evaluated there. The first release does not OCR image-only PDFs or automatically synchronize knowledge between devices.
